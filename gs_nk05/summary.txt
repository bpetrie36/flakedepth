READING
  With a CORRECT forward model (--nk-error 0) the estimator's only failure
  mode is a genuine two-thickness ambiguity: multimodal, and the nuisances
  stay put. The mode-count flag catches those and the oxide gate has nothing
  to see. On real micrographs the reverse holds - failures are unimodal and
  the oxide is displaced - which says the real failures come from model
  misspecification rather than from ambiguity. Raising --nk-error should
  therefore move the synthetic signature toward the real one: mode-flag
  recall falling, oxide-gate recall rising. If it does, the detector's
  mechanism is established. If it does not, the real-data result stands as
  an empirical regularity with its mechanism open, and should be reported
  that way.
