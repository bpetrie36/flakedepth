# Data provenance

## Optical constants
All tabulated n,k from the refractiveindex.info database (CC0):

| Material | Source |
|---|---|
| Si | Aspnes & Studna, Phys. Rev. B 27, 985 (1983) |
| SiO2 | Malitson, J. Opt. Soc. Am. 55, 1205 (1965) — Sellmeier |
| graphene | constant-index model n = 2.6 - 1.3i (Blake et al. 2007) |
| MoS2 | Song et al., Adv. Optical Mater. 7, 1801250 (2019) |
| hBN | Grudinin et al., Mater. Horiz. 10, 2427 (2023), ordinary ray |
| MoSe2 / WS2 / WSe2 | Munkhbat et al., ordinary ray |

Colourimetry: CIE 1931 2-degree standard observer and CIE D65, tabulated.
Illuminant basis: PCA over eleven published illuminant SPDs (A, D50-D75,
LED-B1/B3/B5, FL2/FL7/FL11).

## Evaluation datasets
Real micrographs are the public MaskTerial datasets
(https://zenodo.org/records/15765514), used under their stated licence.
They are not redistributed here. See RUN.md for how to reproduce the
evaluation from the released archives.
